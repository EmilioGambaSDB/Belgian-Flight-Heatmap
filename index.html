    How this works

    Observed layers come from real aircraft positions (ADS-B) recorded by the OpenSky Network. Every day the tracks of all Brussels Airport departures and arrivals are downloaded, and each ~500 m square counts how many flights crossed it below 3,000 m.

    Expected layers are a model of the future: the runway layout, the preferential runway system (which runways are used with which wind, on weekdays, weekends and at night), simplified departure routes and 3° approaches. They show what to expect in general, not exact routes, and do not reflect future rule changes.

    The score (0 to 10) counts flights per day over a spot, weighted by altitude (low counts fully, medium about a third, high a tenth) and with night flights counting ten times, like the official Lden noise measure. It is an indicator, not a noise measurement. Before buying, also check the official noise contours and visit at different times.
